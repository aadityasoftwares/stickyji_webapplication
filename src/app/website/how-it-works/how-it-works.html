Mindful habits

Three daily practices for calmer thinking

Try these simple planning routines and adapt them to your own day.

01

The "Rule of 3 Notes"

Never put more than 3 high-impact notes in your Today column at once. If everything is urgent, nothing is. Finish three notes, then take a breather before pulling from your project boards.

02

The 5-Minute Evening Wind-Down

At the end of your day, spend 5 minutes reviewing your board. Mark finished notes complete, update due dates for remaining work, and shut your laptop knowing tomorrow has a clean, organized starting place.

03

The Brain-Dump Parking Lot

When a distracting random thought arrives mid-deep work, don't context-switch. Create a quick pink note in the "Parking Lot" folder. You protect your flow while preserving the idea.
