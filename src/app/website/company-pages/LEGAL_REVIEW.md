Before relying on these pages for customer acceptance:

- Confirm the legal operator (individual, partnership, company, etc.), complete
  service/postal address, and designated privacy/grievance contact and any legally
  required contact particulars. Confirm that the supplied mailbox is monitored.
- Approve the age restriction, liability cap, business indemnity, jurisdiction,
  cancellation policy, refund eligibility/window, and processing timelines with
  qualified counsel familiar with the business and customer locations.
- Confirm whether each paid plan is a one-time purchase or recurring subscription,
  the actual cancellation path, taxes and invoice disclosures, service delivery,
  and the consequences of cancellation. Razorpay merchant terms require clear
  refund/cancellation disclosures and timelines; the draft is not sufficient for
  that purpose. Show approved details before purchase.
- Inventory backend data processing, hosting regions, subprocessors, email,
  support, analytics, logs, cookies/local storage, advertising, and any AI use.
  Confirm whether information is sold/shared for advertising; do not publish an
  unsupported claim either way. Replace conditional draft wording with accurate
  descriptions of actual purposes, recipients, legal bases, and safeguards.
- Establish and disclose retention periods or clear criteria for each category,
  trash expiry, account/workspace deletion, backups, billing records, and logs.
- Verify security measures and incident response. Do not claim encryption,
  certifications, data residency, or transfer mechanisms without evidence.
- Define processor/controller responsibilities for business workspaces and offer
  a suitable data-processing agreement where required. Review any cross-border
  processing and applicable safeguards.
- Implement rights/grievance handling, identity checks, response deadlines,
  consent withdrawal and non-essential cookie controls as applicable. Verify the
  commencement dates and territorial scope of applicable legislation, including
  India's phased DPDP framework. Do not assume every provision is already in force.
- Record final Terms version, acceptance time, and policy notice version on the
  server. Existing registration sends acceptance booleans only; links added in
  this change do not establish a versioned acceptance audit trail. Treat privacy
  acknowledgement separately from any optional purpose-specific consent.
- Replace all draft passages, set an actual effective date/version, obtain legal
  review, then update COMPANY.policiesApproved and the page metadata/template.
  The template currently says “Draft updated”; change it when approving release.
