Session security notice:

To protect your account, updating your password will immediately sign out all other active sessions across your browsers and devices. You will be redirected to sign in with your new credentials.

Password Best Practices

  • Make it unique: Avoid reusing passwords from personal email, banking, or social media.
  • Use a passphrase: A sequence of 4-5 memorable words is easy to remember and hard to crack.
  • Password managers: Let tools like 1Password, Bitwarden, or Chrome generate and store 16+ character credentials.
  • Beware of phishing: Sticky Ji team members will never ask you for your account password via email or chat.

Session Management

If you suspect unauthorized activity on your account or left yourself signed in on a shared device, you can instantly terminate all active sessions.
